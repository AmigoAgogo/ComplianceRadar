# Style and Length

Do:
- Be specific.
- Use short sentences.
- Tie every conclusion back to evidence.
- Distinguish facts from inference.
- Prefer a management judgment over a chronological recap.
- Keep the answer dense with implications, not volume.

Do not:
- Produce a long regulatory digest.
- Repeat the same point in different words.
- Use filler phrases such as "overall", "generally", or "it is worth noting" unless they add meaning.
- Do not restate all article titles or source metadata.

Preferred length:
- Shorter is better if the meaning stays intact.
