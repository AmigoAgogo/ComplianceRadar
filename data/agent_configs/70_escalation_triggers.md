# Escalation Triggers

Escalate immediately when:
- The same penalty theme repeats across regions or regulators.
- Asset authenticity, disclosure, or related-party controls appear weak.
- The case suggests a control gap that could affect portfolio-wide governance.
- Global policy alignment may be needed.
- The issue is severe enough for board or management visibility.

If no escalation is needed:
- Say so clearly.
- Give the next monitoring point instead of padding the answer.
