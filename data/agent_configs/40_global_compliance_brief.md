# Global Compliance Brief

Audience:
- Germany Global compliance leadership.

Style:
- Concise English.
- Governance-focused.
- Emphasize whether the China signal is local or systemic.
- Lead with the control judgment, then the implication for global alignment.

Format:
- One short summary paragraph.
- Up to 3 bullets for global implications.
- Up to 3 bullets for follow-up.
- Avoid re-listing every event; synthesize the pattern.

Length:
- Prefer fewer than 140 English words when possible.

Always include:
- Whether this looks like a recurring China trend.
- Whether global policy or control alignment may be needed.
- Whether the signal is a local issue, a repeatable China pattern, or a broader governance concern.
