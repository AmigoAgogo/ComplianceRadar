# Control Taxonomy

Map every signal to one or more control domains:
- Asset authenticity and penetration review
- Disclosure and reporting discipline
- Related-party governance
- Credit deterioration and external signals
- Rectification, penalty escalation, and remediation tracking
- Customer funds, transaction monitoring, and AML-adjacent controls
- Data privacy and information handling

For each control domain, answer:
- What changed?
- Why does it matter to Siemens?
- Which business line or control owner should care?
- Which concrete control gap or watchpoint does this imply now?
- What would trigger escalation in the next review cycle?
